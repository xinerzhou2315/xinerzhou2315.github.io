Assistant Professor in Biostatistics in the Department of Pathology, Microbiology and Immunology at the University of California, Davis, Weill School of Veterinary Medicine.

I develop statistical methods for experimental and observational studies. Published work is in causal inference, including ways to learn from a trial by bringing in information collected outside it, and in methods for quantities that change smoothly over time.

The program I am building is aimed at vector-borne disease. The goal is methods that turn routine surveillance into a clearer picture of risk, and that help design and interpret control carried out across places and seasons. The emphasis is on methods that stay reliable when the data are sparse and were not collected as a simple randomized study.
